# Baccarat Mathematics

## Basic Probabilities

- **Banker Win**: ~45.86%
- **Player Win**: ~44.62%
- **Tie**: ~9.52%

## House Edge

- Banker bet: 1.06%
- Player bet: 1.24%
- Tie bet: 14.4%
